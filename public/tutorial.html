Step 1: What is Nim

Nim is a strategy game with rows of stones. Two players take turns. On your turn, choose one row and remove at least 1 stone from that row. The player who takes the last stone wins.

Step 3: The Secret Nim-Sum

Nim strategy uses XOR (nim-sum). Convert each heap to binary and XOR them.

Heap Binary
1 001
3 011
5 101
7 111
XOR Result 000

001 XOR 011 XOR 101 XOR 111 = 000

If the nim-sum is 0, you are in a losing position against perfect play. If it is not 0, there is a winning move.

Step 4: Finding the Winning Move

Example heaps: 1, 3, 5, 6. Their nim-sum is 1. For each heap, compute heap XOR nim-sum. If the result is less than the heap, reducing to that value is a winning move.

1 XOR 1 = 0 (less, valid move)
3 XOR 1 = 2 (less, valid move)
5 XOR 1 = 4 (less, valid move)
6 XOR 1 = 5 (less, winning move)

One strong move is to take 1 from the heap of 6 to leave 5, which makes the new nim-sum 0.
